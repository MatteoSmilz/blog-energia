Il Sole converte massa in energia secondo $E = mc^2$ [@einstein1905].

$$
P(E) \propto e^{-\sqrt{E_G / E}}
$$

$$
\Delta T_{\text{teorico}} = \frac{-Q}{m_{\text{tot}} \cdot c} = \frac{-n_{\text{sale}} \cdot \Delta H^\circ_{\text{sol}}}{(m_{\text{acqua}} + m_{\text{sale}}) \cdot c} = \frac{-\dfrac{m_{\text{sale}}}{M_{\text{sale}}} \cdot \Delta H^\circ_{\text{sol}}}{(m_{\text{acqua}} + m_{\text{sale}}) \cdot c} = \frac{-\dfrac{7{,}56\ \text{g}}{101{,}10\ \text{g/mol}} \cdot 34\,900\ \text{J/mol}}{(50{,}0\ \text{g} + 7{,}56\ \text{g}) \cdot 3{,}76\ \text{J/(g·K)}} \approx -12{,}1\ \text{K}
$$
